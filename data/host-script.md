# The host script — running a Read My Nails session
The whole business in one rule: **you teach, she does.** Your hands never touch hers.

## The kiosk setup (two stations + one printer)
- **Station A and Station B:** a mat, a UV/LED lamp, base gel, print gel (white PG4 and clear PG0), nail mask, top gel, cleanser pads, lint-free wipes, a small trash cup, a plastic practice tip on a stick. Buy **two** lamps, not one — two customers prep while a third prints.
- **The printer** sits between the stations with the tablet facing the customer.
- **On the counter:** the price board, the "You print it. We teach you." sign, the allergy card, the session binder (parent cards, day sheet backup).
- **Your tools:** a plastic tip on a stick for demonstrating every step. You demo on the tip. She copies on her nail.

## The 12-minute session, one hand (say it in this order)
1. **Welcome (30 s).** "Hi! I'm the host. Today you're going to print your own nails — I'll show you every step. Pick a set on the screen or build your own." She picks, gets her code on the app, shows you.
2. **Look, don't touch (20 s).** Ask her to hold up her hand. Look for existing gel/acrylic, broken skin, anything you wouldn't want gel on. Existing product → "Let's do it on a press-on tip today, it'll look amazing." Broken skin → tip on that finger, or skip it. You never touch the hand to check.
3. **Cleanse (30 s).** Hand her a cleanser pad. "Wipe each nail like this." Demo on the tip.
4. **Base (1 min).** "Thin coat, stay off the skin, like coloring inside the lines." Demo on the tip. She brushes. "Anything on skin? Wipe it with the stick before the lamp." **Lamp 30–60 s.** She puts her own hand in.
5. **Mask (30 s).** "Paint the skin around the nail — this is what keeps ink off your finger." Demo. She paints. Let it dry to a film.
6. **Print gel (45 s).** White for pale/clear nails, clear for polished color. "Thin coat, do not cure." This is the layer the ink sticks to.
7. **Print (about 40 s a nail).** You tap the five tiles in order on the tablet — that's the machine, not her nails. "Put your finger on the rest, nail flat, hold still. It's just a camera and a tiny inkjet." She places each finger. Photo nail: crop it square on the app first.
8. **Peel and cure (1 min).** "Peel the mask off like a sticker." She peels. **Lamp** to set the ink.
9. **Top coat (1 min).** "Thin coat, seal the tip edge." She brushes. **Lamp 60 s.**
10. **Reveal (30 s).** "Hold it up. Can you read it?" Photo for her, photo for the wall if she says yes (parent says yes for a minor).
11. **Send-off (30 s).** "It lasts about two weeks. No picking. When you're done, soak it off at home — never peel it. Bring a friend for a Duo, you both save."

Both hands ≈ 20 minutes. Group workshop: run stations in rotation, 3 girls in motion at once.

## What you say when it goes wrong
- She smudges a nail: "Wipe it off with the pad and let's do that one again." (She wipes.)
- Gel on the skin: "Grab the stick and clear it off before the lamp." (She clears.)
- Print off-center: "Wipe it, one more thin coat of print gel, we'll run it again." Reprints are pennies.
- She can't hold still (small kids): "Mom, can you help her hold her hand on the rest?" Parent helps, not you.
- "Can you just do it for me?" — "I'm not allowed to do your nails — I'm not a nail tech and this isn't a salon. But I promise you can do this, watch the tip."

## Never
- Never touch a customer's hand, finger, or nail. Not to steady, not to fix, not to check.
- Never file, buff, cut, push cuticles, or remove anything.
- Never say "service," "manicure," "I'll do your nails," "nail tech," "salon."
- Never ask about allergies or medical conditions — the allergy card on the counter does that job.
- Never keep a customer's photo after the session unless she saved her order in the app.

## Under-18 rule
- Under 10: a parent or guardian does the brushing with the child; the child places her own finger in the printer.
- 10–17: parent present, or parent signs the session card in the binder (one line: name, date, "my child may take a self-service nail printing session").

## Day sheet
Every session is logged in the app (Host tab → Done). If the tablet dies, the paper day sheet in the binder: time, code, session type, price, EN/ES. Enter it into the app that night. After 30 days we cut the bottom 20% of sets and write 50 new ones from what the girls actually asked for.

## Spanish
Every line above has a Spanish version on the laminated card at each station. The app switches with one tap (Español). Say the price in both languages on the board; say the steps in whichever language she speaks first.
