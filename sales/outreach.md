## The free three sets
Send `sample-sets.zip`: 15 tiles (three sets: one puzzle, one occasion, one Spanish), the price board PDF, and a one-page "how a session runs." Anyone who prints them at the counter and watches a customer react is a customer. Keep it to three; the library is the product.

## What to say when they ask

- **"Does it work with my printer?"** Any inkjet nail printer that loads your own PNG. O'2NAILS X11 Plus, X12.5, V11, Jolimark. If theirs only shows the built-in gallery, it doesn't, and say so.
- **"Do I need a license to do this?"** The session model keeps the host's hands off the customer. It's the position we run on in Florida; they check with their own state board. Never say "you don't need a license" to someone in another state.
- **"Can I see the whole library first?"** No — three sets free, then the book on the dashboard. The library is what they're paying for.
- **"Is there a cheap way in?"** The Start pack: five chapters for $29, print tonight. The Salon plan ($59/mo) adds the app, the generator and two chapters a month.
- **"Two locations?"** Two licenses. The second one gets its own kiosk address.

## Weekly rhythm (2 hours)
Monday: post in two groups. Tuesday–Thursday: 10 DMs a day to salons posting printer content. Friday: reply to everything, send sample sets, log who's in the sheet. Every new salon: ask for a photo of a real hand and post it.

## Track it
One sheet: salon, city, printer model, where you found them, date of first message, sample sent?, signed up?, note. When 20 messages produce zero replies, change the message, not the channel.
